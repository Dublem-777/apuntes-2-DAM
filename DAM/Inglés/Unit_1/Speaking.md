
# Software requirements for Transmore

## Contexto
Transmore es un taller de reparación de aparatos eléctricos. Necesita software para gestionar pagos, seguir el progreso de cada trabajo, controlar los cambios de stock y mantener una base de datos de clientes. La empresa quiere usar el software durante al menos tres años, probar una versión demo durante 30 días y, más adelante, añadir módulos para expandirse al mercado minorista. También necesita asistencia técnica telefónica 24/7.

## Speaking: Choosing software for Transmore

**A — Software vendor:** Good morning. What kind of software are you looking for?

**B — Transmore representative:** We run an electrical repair shop and need software to take payments, track each customer’s job, monitor stock levels and maintain a customer database.

**A:** Our system can do all that, and you can customise it to improve your company’s efficiency. Are you looking for a long-term licence?

**B:** Yes, for at least three years. We also plan to expand into retail, so we’ll need additional modules that work with our existing database. Do you offer a demo?

**A:** Yes. You can apply for a free 30-day trial version on our website. I’ll also send you a brochure. The software is available with a three-year licence or a monthly subscription.

**B:** Great. We also need a 24/7 support helpline, plus maintenance and help configuring the software.

**A:** Those are included for business customers. We can also help with future modules and add-ons.

**B:** Excellent. Please send me the brochure and the link to apply for the trial. Thank you for your help.

**A:** You’re welcome. I’ll send them this afternoon.

## Vocabulario de la unidad utilizado

- **apply for a trial version** — solicitar una versión de prueba
- **brochure** — folleto
- **configure** — configurar
- **customise** — personalizar
- **database** — base de datos
- **efficiency** — eficiencia
- **existing** — existente
- **licence** — licencia
- **maintenance** — mantenimiento
- **module / add-on** — módulo / complemento
- **subscription** — suscripción
- **track** — seguir/monitorizar
- **vendor** — vendedor/proveedor
