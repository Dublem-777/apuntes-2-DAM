## 1. Copy and complete the sentences

> 2. You can download a free t__________ version and test the program for 30 days.
> 3. The company pays a monthly s__________ to use the software.
> 4. All the information about our customers is stored in a d__________. 
> 5. We use a p__________ program to pay the employees' salaries. payroll
> 6. The v__________ sent us a b__________ with the prices of the software. vendor. brochure
> 7. You can c__________ the software so it fits your company's needs.

## 4. Parts of Word

> [!question] Name the part of Word
> 1. It shows the page number, word count and language.
> 2. The large toolbar that contains tabs and commands.
> 3. It shows the program name and the title of the document.
> 4. The sections on the ribbon, like Home, Insert or Layout.

## 6. Grammar

> [!question] 6.1 First conditional: put the verbs in the correct form
> 1. If we __________ (use) online tools, it __________ (be) easier to work together.
> 2. It __________ (be) clearer if we __________ (filter) the emails from this week.
> 3. If you __________ (not / save) the file, you __________ (lose) your work.
> 4. The client __________ (be) happy if we __________ (send) the draft on time.

> [!success]- Solution
> 1. use / will be · 2. will be / filter · 3. don't save / will lose · 4. will be / send
>
> *Regla:* **If + presente**, **will + verbo**. Nunca *will* detrás de *if*.

> [!question] 6.2 Circle the correct pronoun
> 1. This laptop is (my / mine).
> 2. Can you send (they / them) the brochure?
> 3. Is this phone (her / hers)?
> 4. (Our / Ours) company needs new software.
> 5. The program is great, but (its / it's) licence is expensive.
> 6. The manager called (we / us) yesterday.

> [!success]- Solution
> 1. mine · 2. them · 3. hers · 4. Our · 5. its · 6. us

> [!question] 6.3 Complete with who, which, that, where or when
> 1. The developer __________ designed the website is very good.
> 2. This is the office __________ we work.
> 3. Friday is the day __________ the client reviews the advert.
> 4. These are the diagrams __________ show the sales.
> 5. He's the person __________ / __________ sent the email. (dos opciones)

> [!success]- Solution
> 1. who · 2. where · 3. when · 4. which (o that) · 5. who / that

## 7. Reading

> [!question] Read the text and answer the questions
> *Transmore is an electrical repair shop. It needs software to take payments, track the progress of each job, monitor stock levels and keep a customer database. The company wants to use the software for at least three years and test a free 30-day demo first. Later, it plans to add modules to expand into the retail market. It also needs a 24/7 telephone helpline.*
>
> 1. What kind of company is Transmore?
> 2. Name three things the software must do.
> 3. How long does Transmore want to use the software?
> 4. What does it want to do before buying?
> 5. Why will it need extra modules in the future?
> 6. True or false: Transmore only needs support during office hours.

> [!success]- Solution
> 1. An electrical repair shop.
> 2. Take payments, track each job, monitor stock levels, keep a customer database (any three).
> 3. At least three years.
> 4. Test a free 30-day demo / trial version.
> 5. To expand into the retail market.
> 6. False — it needs a 24/7 helpline.
