## 4. Gramática

> [!note] No aparece en tu lista del examen, pero se dio en clase y suele caer en los *complete the sentences*.

### 4.1 First conditional

> [!definition] Estructura
> **If + presente simple, will + verbo**. Las dos partes se pueden invertir.

- *If we **use** a spreadsheet, it **will be** easier to plan the budget.*
- *It **will be** quicker if we **sort** the emails by date or sender.*
- *If I **configure** the software, it **will work** with our database.*

### 4.2 Pronombres

| Sujeto | Objeto | Adjetivo posesivo (+ nombre) | Pronombre posesivo (solo) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | me | my | mine |
| you | you | your | yours |
| he | him | his | his |
| she | her | her | hers |
| it | it | its | — |
| we | us | our | ours |
| they | them | their | theirs |

- *This is my laptop* → *This laptop is **mine**.*
- *These are their brochures* → *These brochures are **theirs**.*

> [!warning] Trampa
> **its** (posesivo) no lleva apóstrofo; **it's** = *it is*.

### 4.3 Relative pronouns

| Pronombre | Se usa para | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| who | personas | the person **who** designed it |
| which | cosas | the diagrams **which** show the data |
| that | personas o cosas | the results **that** we got |
| where | lugares | the company **where** I work |
| when | momentos | the time **when** the meeting starts |

## 5. Reading y listening

### 5.1 Tipos de software (reading Unit 1)
- **System software**: el sistema operativo y los *system management programs*.
- **Application software**: *general-purpose* (Word, Excel, presentaciones) y *application-specific* (nóminas, contabilidad, ventas).

### 5.2 ¿Quién dice cada frase?

> [!tip] Truco
> El **cliente** pide algo o se queja; el **developer / vendor / graphic designer** ofrece y pregunta.

- Cliente: *I need the logo to be more obvious.* · *The sign is too small for people to read.* · *Can you send it to me by Thursday afternoon?*
- Diseñador / vendedor: *Have you got any comments on the first draft?* · *What would you like me to change?* · *Are you looking for a long-term licence?*

### 5.3 Frases útiles (speaking Transmore)
- *You can apply for a free 30-day trial version on our website.*
- *It's available with a three-year licence or a monthly subscription.*
- *We need a 24/7 support helpline, plus maintenance.*
- *We'll need additional modules that work with our existing database.*
